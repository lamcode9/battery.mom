Battery.mom · Phase 0 gate deliverable · v1 — for KM's review before any code

One Cinematic Work

Storyboard and site architecture for turning battery.mom from a site with a cinematic page into a single cinematic argument: the physics changed, so the economics are changing, so the smart move is now the beautiful move — and here are the numbers and tools to act on it.

Two corrections drive everything below. First: the video is the stage, not an exhibit — full-bleed, behind the words, edge to edge. The FilmCel picture-in-a-box dies. Second: /sunrise is not a destination, it is the spine — every other page becomes a chapter of evidence hanging off it, sharing one design language.

01 · The film grammar

How a scene works

Each act becomes a scene: a full-viewport stage pinned behind the scroll. The loop video fills the stage. The act's words and data scroll over it in a left-anchored reading column. The reader is never looking at footage; they are standing in it.

subject lives here
(right two-thirds)
the anatomy of every scene

The standing composition: reading column in the left third over a directional scrim; the shot's subject weighted right; a soft floor scrim so the column never collides with bright ground. Film grain (already built) lies over both video and canvas so they read as one emulsion.

The five laws of the stage
  • Full bleed, always. No frames, no hairline chrome, no caption strips, no aspect-ratio boxes. A caption, when needed, is a film subtitle: small italic serif, bottom-right, on the footage itself.
  • The words live in the shot. Legibility comes from the directional scrim + grain + type weight — never from putting the text on a card. Contrast stays ≥4.5:1 over the darkest-case frame (scrims are tuned per shot, and we verify on real frames, not on the poster).
  • Every act boundary is an authored cut. Scroll position drives a crossfade/match-cut between stages (~120vh of overlap). The title-card choreography we built in v3 (rule draws, era slides, masked numeral/title rise) survives — but it now plays over the incoming footage, which is what it was always for.
  • Data reads in reading light. When a chart, ladder, or big-number figure enters, the stage dims to ≈25–35% and settles (video keeps looping, felt not watched). Charts never sit on busy footage. The figure chrome (hairline rules, italic serif labels) stays — it's the film's title-graphics language.
  • The sky is the score, the video is the shot. The canvas sunrise (night → coal → false dawn → first light → space → morning) remains the master light score for the whole scroll: it owns the color temperature, fills every gap between scenes, and is the zero-asset fallback. When a scene has footage, the footage wins the frame and the canvas dims behind it. They never fight because the video's grade is locked to the sky's keyframe at that story position (grade hexes per scene, below).

SceneStage — the one new primitive

A single fixed full-viewport layer that owns the background. Scenes register their scroll ranges; the stage crossfades between (at most two mounted) videos, drives the dim level for data beats, and hands the frame back to the canvas between scenes. Missing asset → that scene simply plays on canvas (today's zero-asset behavior, now by architecture instead of by hiding). Reduced motion → full-bleed poster stills, same scrims, same composition — the film becomes a photo essay, not a different page. Mobile: same grammar; shots are center/right-weighted so a 9:16 center-crop keeps the subject and the text column gains a slightly stronger scrim.

Performance: only the active + next scene mount video. Clips re-encoded for full-screen (1080p, CRF ~20, target ≤3.5 MB each, ~8 MB total above-the-fold budget unchanged because loading is sequential and lazy). preload=metadata beyond the first scene.

03 · The site as one work

The morning the story promises

The unifying conceit — free, already latent in what exists: the story ends at morning on paper, and every other page on battery.mom is that morning. Narrative surfaces live in the film's night grades; tool and data surfaces live in its daylight. Same emulsion (grain), same type, same chart language, same hairline title-graphics — one film, two times of day. Nothing needs a costume; everything needs the same tailor.

Cross-links: handoffs at the moment of belief

The story should hand the reader to a tool at the exact beat where the claim lands, not in a card dump at the end:

  • Act III's SEA inset ("that is why battery.mom starts here") → inline link into /state-of-battery-power.
  • Act III's storage beat → /scoreboard/energy ("watch the GWh fill in").
  • Act V's six gates → the zero-bill planner, phrased as the first gate the reader personally controls. The existing three cards stay as the formal ending.
  • Reverse direction: proof/tool pages get one quiet standing line — "Part of The Long Sunrise — the whole argument, from the first fire" — replacing generic breadcrumbs.

06 · The asset plan

Reshoot for the full frame

The 9 existing clips were art-directed for framed boxes. Full-bleed changes the brief in four ways, and the Grok prompt doc (docs/sunrise-asset-prompts.html) gets rewritten around them before you generate:

  1. Composition: subject weighted to the right two-thirds; left third and bottom quarter kept calm/dark for the reading column and floor scrim. This is the biggest change — none of the current prompts asked for it.
  2. Loop: explicit seamless-loop instruction (end frame ≈ start frame; no entering/exiting objects), locked camera or ≤5% drift. Full-screen makes loop seams 10× more visible than a box did.
  3. Scale: generate at the highest resolution offered, 16:9 only; we encode at 1080p. Center/right-weighted subjects so the mobile 9:16 center-crop survives.
  4. Grade: same per-act hexes (now printed per scene above) — but graded to sit against the canvas sky at that story position, since the two now share the frame edge during cuts.

Expected outcome: first-light, megablock, gigafactory, orbit almost certainly need retakes (subject-centered today); fire, combustion, false-dawn, swarm, morning get audited against the three gates first — the winners you picked may already hold the full frame. Real-hardware anchors (Starlink V3, Powerwall 3, Megapack, "resembling"/no-logos) all carry over.
